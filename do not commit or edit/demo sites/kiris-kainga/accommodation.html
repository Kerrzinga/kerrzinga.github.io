Your Resting Place

Three rooms, each named for a native tree — each a sanctuary woven with warmth, timber, and the spirit of Aotearoa.

Nohanga — Accommodation

Comfort Meets Culture

At Kiri's Kāinga, your room is more than a place to sleep. Each space has been thoughtfully designed to reflect the textures and tones of the natural world around us — recycled tōtara timber, handwoven tukutuku panels, and linen in the colours of sea and soil. Guests are welcome to use the shared garden kitchen, native plant library, and fire pit at all hours.

Garden Suite Most Popular
Room One

Pōhutukawa Suite

Named for the scarlet-flowering coastal tree that lines New Zealand's shores, the Pōhutukawa Suite opens onto a secluded garden deck where you can watch fantails dart through harakeke (flax) at dawn. Inside, hand-stitched tapa-cloth cushions, warm timber floors, and a king bed draped in natural linen create an atmosphere of deep, grounded calm.

Room Two

Tōtara Room

The tōtara is one of New Zealand's most sacred and enduring trees — and this room carries that same quiet strength. Elevated to catch the sea breeze, the Tōtara Room features a private verandah overlooking the Hauraki Gulf, a hand-carved pou (post) in the corner, a queen bed with merino-wool throws, and a rainfall shower clad in natural stone. Wake up and watch the sun rise from the water.

🛏 Queen Bed 🚿 Stone Rainfall Shower 🌊 Sea Views 🪵 Hand-carved Pou 📶 Free WiFi 🎨 Original Māori Art
NZD $245 / night (2 guests)

Includes full cooked breakfast & a guided 30-min kōrero (conversation)

Room Three

Kauri Wharepuni

The Kauri Wharepuni is a full self-contained dwelling — a traditional sleeping house reinterpreted for modern families. Reclaimed kauri timber runs throughout. There's a master bedroom with queen bed, two single bunks in the children's nook, a full kitchen stocked with local produce, and a wraparound porch from which to watch the stars. It's a place to be together, unhurried.

🛏 Queen + 2 Bunks 🍳 Full Kitchen 🏡 Private Porch 🔥 Fire Pit Access 📶 Free WiFi 🎒 Kids Welcome
NZD $320 / night (up to 5 guests)

Includes breakfast provisions & one family cultural session
