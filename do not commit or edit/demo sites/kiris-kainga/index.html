More Than a Place to Sleep

Kiri's Kāinga is a living, breathing expression of manaakitanga — the Māori art of hospitality and care for others. Nestled on the Whangaparaoa Peninsula with views stretching to the Hauraki Gulf, our homestay offers a rare chance to connect with Māori culture from the inside.

Host Kiri Tūhoe grew up on this land, surrounded by the stories of her tīpuna (ancestors). She now shares those stories — through guided walks along ancient pathways, evening hāngi feasts, and hands-on cultural sessions — with visitors from around the world.

Stories That Stay With You

Immerse yourself in the living traditions of Māori culture — guided by those who carry them forward.

🌿

Guided Bush Walk

Follow ancient pāua pathways through native bush, learning about rongoā (medicinal plants) and local whakapapa.
