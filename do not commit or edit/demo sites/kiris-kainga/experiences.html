Wheako — Experiences

Stories, Kai & Connection

Every experience at Kiri's Kāinga is co-created with local hapū (sub-tribe) members, elders, and tohunga (experts). We believe that the most powerful travel moments happen when you're not a tourist — but a temporary member of a living community. All experiences can be booked as add-ons to your stay or as standalone day visits.

Experience One

Guided Bush Walk
Haerenga i te ngahere

⏱ 2–3 hours 👥 2–12 guests 📅 Daily at 8am & 2pm 🥾 Moderate pace

Follow Kiri along ancient pathways through native podocarps and along coastal headlands the same routes her ancestors walked for hundreds of years. Along the way she'll introduce you to the rongoā (medicinal plants) tucked between the ferns: kawakawa for healing, tī kōuka for strength, mānuka for everything.

You'll pause at a sacred grove where Kiri shares the whakapapa (genealogy) of the land — how each hill, stream, and rock formation carries a name, a story, and a relationship. This isn't a history lecture; it's a conversation between the past and the present.

What's included:

  • Expert guided narration by Kiri or a trained whānau guide
  • Herbal tea tasting from foraged native plants
  • A small printed field guide of plants encountered
  • Morning tea on the clifftop overlooking the Gulf
NZD $85 per person

Free for accommodation guests staying 2+ nights

Experience Two

Hāngi Evening
Te kai o te whenua

⏱ All day (prep from 6am) 👥 Up to 20 guests 📅 Fridays & Saturdays 🔥 Earth oven cooking

The hāngi is the oldest and most sacred cooking method in Māori culture — and experiencing one at Kiri's Kāinga is unlike anything else. At first light, volcanic stones are heated in a fire for several hours. Then the kai (food) — marinated chicken, lamb, whole kūmara (sweet potato), vegetables, and stuffing wrapped in harakeke baskets — is layered into the earth.

Guests are welcome to watch the morning preparation, help weave the food baskets, and join the ceremonial lowering of the kai into the pit. As the earth gives back its gift at sunset, the whole whānau gathers around long tables for a feast accompanied by waiata (songs) and storytelling under the open sky.

What's included:

  • Full hāngi feast (meat, vegetables, kūmara, rewena bread)
  • Participation in preparation and the ceremony
  • Evening of waiata and kōrero (storytelling) by the fire
  • Non-alcoholic welcome drink on arrival
  • Vegetarian & dietary alternatives available on request
NZD $135 per person

Discount of 20% for accommodation guests. Bookings essential — minimum 6 guests.

Experience Three

Kapa Haka Intro
Ko te haka, ko te tangata

⏱ 90 minutes 👥 All welcome 📅 Tues, Thurs, Sun 5pm 🎵 Beginners welcome

Kapa haka is the heartbeat of Māori identity — a powerful blend of posture, voice, rhythm, and meaning that has carried cultural knowledge across generations. In this 90-minute introductory session, Kiri's cousin Aroha leads guests through the basic elements: the haka (posture dance), waiata-ā-ringa (action songs), and the principles of unity and presence that underpin every performance.

No performance experience is needed — only a willingness to try. The session ends with a simple group haka that guests perform together. Many describe it as unexpectedly emotional: there is something in the act of voice, movement, and breath shared with others that transcends language.

What's included:

  • Introduction to kapa haka history and meaning
  • Guided learning of a waiata and basic haka sequence
  • Group practice and a final group performance
  • Short Q&A with Aroha on the place of kapa haka today
  • A printed karakia (blessing) to take home
NZD $65 per person

Children under 12 free when accompanied by a paying adult.
