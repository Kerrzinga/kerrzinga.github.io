A Hibiscus Coast family is seeking witnesses after their 13-year-old son was allegedly stood over and robbed at Orewa train station at around 6pm, with the boy's shoes taken by a group of older teenagers.

The mother posted to the community group saying the perpetrators — believed to be around 17 years old — had not been held accountable, with the security guard on duty allegedly taking no action.

"His friends are saying nothing," she wrote in her post, which quickly attracted more than 100 reactions and 95 comments.

In a follow-up, she indicated that CCTV footage had been obtained and that she had identified the main suspect along with two others seen with him. The update, posted in all caps, read: "WAITING ON CCTV TO BE RELEASED TODAY. I HAVE PHOTOS AND NAMES IDENTIFYING THE MAIN SUSPECT AND 2 OTHERS SEEN WITH HIM."

The incident has sparked concern among local parents about safety at the station during after-school hours. Community members in the comments called on AT (Auckland Transport) and station management to review security arrangements.

Anyone with information is encouraged to contact NZ Police on 105.
