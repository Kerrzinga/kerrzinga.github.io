Sometimes the smallest offers of kindness make the biggest impression — and this week, a simple attempt to help a stranger in the rain went quietly viral in the Hibiscus Coast community.

Delwyn Clerke posted to the group hoping to track down a woman she had spotted walking her two young children to Whangaparaoa Primary on a wet morning, umbrella blown inside out by the wind.

"I tried to call out to you to offer an umbrella or a ride to school," Delwyn wrote. "Weather is supposed to be bad again tomorrow. Please DM me if you'd like a lift to school tomorrow morning; I'm assuming you're local and I'd love to help you."

The post drew 203 reactions and 18 comments, with locals flooding the thread with heart emojis and replies like "this is what community is all about."

It's the kind of moment that reminds us why local Facebook groups — amid the noise — still have something irreplaceable to offer.
