A Hibiscus Coast man's work vehicle was stolen, driven to Fisherman's Cove, and set alight this week — leaving him without the tools he relies on for his livelihood. The story drew an outpouring of community support, with hundreds of locals reacting and a fundraiser quickly gaining traction.

## The Theft

Matt Kinchley left his 2000 Hilux Surf (plates ATN674) in the 4 Square carpark overnight earlier this week, having made the responsible decision not to drive after drinking. When he returned the next morning, the vehicle was gone.

"I'm extremely stressed out as all of my work tools are in the back along with some pretty sentimental items," he posted to the group. "If anyone has any CCTV footage of nearby roads or anything please please reach out as I'm desperate to get my car back."

The post received 46 reactions and 14 comments as the community helped spread the word.

## Found — and Torched

The following day, the news took a grim turn. The Hilux was discovered on the rocks at Fisherman's Cove, completely burnt out. Everything inside — tools, personal belongings — had been removed and dumped on the beach, left for the tide.

"Update about my stolen Hilux surf — it has been found on the rocks of Fisherman's Cove, burnt, everything inside including all of my tools was dumped on the beach left for the tide to ruin everything," Matt wrote. "Huge thank you to the people who helped me out today to find the car and for all the kind messages. Much love to our wonderful community here on the Hibiscus Coast."

That update drew 431 reactions and 78 comments — a reflection of how widely the community had rallied around him.

## Fundraiser Launched

In a final update, Matt announced a GiveALittle fundraiser had been set up at the request of community members, as his tools were uninsured.

"Please do not feel pressured to donate as I know times are tough for a lot of us right now," he wrote. "Anything helps a lot and will be used to get me back on my feet and back to doing what I love the most, fixing and building cars everyday. Thank you all so much for your support."

The fundraiser can be found at [givealittle.co.nz](https://givealittle.co.nz) — search "Stolen and Tourched Hilux."
