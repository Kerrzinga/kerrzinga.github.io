A local parent is urging Hibiscus Coast families to talk to their kids about e-scooter safety, following a near-miss incident outside Orewa College on Friday afternoon.

Around 4:30pm, a young boy riding an e-scooter ran into the back of a car at the pedestrian crossing on the road outside the college. The driver, who posted about the incident, said she was relieved he hadn't appeared seriously hurt — and that the impact hadn't been the other way around.

"We're really relieved it wasn't the other way around and that he didn't appear seriously hurt," she wrote. "However, this situation made me realise how risky e-scooters can be, especially when they're being ridden on the road. They're fast and don't offer the same protection as a car, so accidents can happen very quickly and can be devastating."

The driver said she wasn't posting to "be a Karen" but to raise awareness, noting that the pair didn't get the chance to exchange details at the time. She appealed to anyone who may have witnessed the incident or knows the young person involved to get in touch.

The post struck a chord with the community, drawing 89 reactions and 29 comments.

Parents are encouraged to remind children that e-scooters are subject to the same road rules as cyclists, and that riding on footpaths and pedestrian crossings requires extra caution around vehicles.
