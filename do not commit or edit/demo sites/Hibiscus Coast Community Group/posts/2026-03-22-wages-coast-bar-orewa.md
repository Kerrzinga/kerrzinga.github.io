A Hibiscus Coast parent has taken to the community Facebook group to raise concerns about wages allegedly not being paid to young workers at a local hospitality venue, prompting dozens of responses from others who say they have had similar experiences.

Rachael Buckingham posted asking if any other parents had children who were working — or had previously worked — at Coast Bar / Oliver's in Orewa.

"I have some serious concerns around wages not being paid despite being promised," she wrote. "It's really unfair and also illegal to not be paid, and from what I am hearing this is not an uncommon theme from this business."

The post received 47 reactions and 34 comments, with a number of community members responding to say they had encountered similar issues.

Under New Zealand employment law, all workers — including young people and those employed on a casual basis — are entitled to at least the minimum wage for all hours worked, and employers are required to keep accurate wage records. Workers who believe they have been underpaid can contact Employment New Zealand's free helpline on 0800 20 90 20, or lodge a complaint online at [employment.govt.nz](https://www.employment.govt.nz).
