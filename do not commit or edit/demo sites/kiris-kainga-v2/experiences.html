Stories, Kai
& Connection

Every experience at Kiri's Kāinga is co-created with local hapū members, elders, and tohunga. The most powerful travel moments happen when you're not a tourist — but a temporary member of a living community. All experiences can be booked as add-ons to a stay or as standalone day visits.

Experience 01

Guided Bush
Walk

Haerenga i te ngahere — Journey into the forest

Follow Kiri along ancient pathways through native podocarps and coastal headlands — the same routes her ancestors walked for centuries. She'll introduce you to the rongoā (medicinal plants) hidden between the ferns: kawakawa for healing, tī kōuka for strength, mānuka for everything. You'll pause at a sacred grove where she shares the whakapapa of the land — how every hill, stream, and rock carries a name, a story, and a relationship.

⏱ 2–3 hours 👥 2–12 guests 📅 Daily 8am & 2pm 🥾 Moderate pace

Included

  • Expert guided narration by Kiri or trained whānau guide
  • Herbal tea tasting from foraged native plants
  • Printed field guide of plants encountered
  • Morning tea on the clifftop above the Gulf
NZD $85
Per person · Free for accommodation guests staying 2+ nights

Hāngi
Evening

Te kai o te whenua — The food of the earth

At first light, volcanic stones are heated in a fire for hours. Then the kai — marinated chicken, lamb, whole kūmara, vegetables — is layered into the earth in harakeke baskets. Guests are welcome to watch the morning preparation, help weave the baskets, and join the ceremonial lowering of the food into the pit. At sunset, the whole whānau gathers around long tables for a feast accompanied by waiata and storytelling beneath open sky.

⏱ All day (from 6am) 👥 Up to 20 guests 📅 Fri & Sat 🔥 Earth oven

Included

  • Full hāngi feast — meat, kūmara, vegetables, rewena bread
  • Participation in preparation and the ceremony
  • Evening of waiata and kōrero by the fire
  • Vegetarian alternatives available on request
NZD $135
Per person · 20% discount for accommodation guests · Min. 6 guests

Experience 03

Kapa Haka
Intro

Ko te haka, ko te tangata — The haka is the person

Kapa haka is the heartbeat of Māori identity — a powerful blend of posture, voice, rhythm, and meaning that has carried cultural knowledge across generations. Led by Kiri's cousin Aroha, this 90-minute session takes you through the haka, waiata-ā-ringa, and the principles of unity and presence. No experience needed — only willingness. The session ends with a group haka many guests describe as unexpectedly and profoundly moving.

⏱ 90 minutes 👥 All welcome 📅 Tue, Thu, Sun 5pm 🎵 Beginners welcome

Included

  • History and meaning of kapa haka
  • Guided waiata and haka sequence
  • Group practice and final performance
  • Printed karakia (blessing) to take home
NZD $65
Per person · Children under 12 free with paying adult
