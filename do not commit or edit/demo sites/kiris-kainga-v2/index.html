More than a
place to sleep

Kiri's Kāinga is a living expression of manaakitanga — the Māori art of hospitality and care. Nestled on the Whangaparaoa Peninsula with views to the Hauraki Gulf, we offer something rare: a genuine, unhurried window into Māori life, land, and story.

Host Kiri Tūhoe grew up on this land. She shares it with guests from 40+ countries through guided walks on ancient pathways, hāngi feasts cooked in the earth, and evenings of song around the fire.
