The service

Coastie Websites designs, builds, hosts, and maintains websites for New Zealand businesses. For $95 per month (NZD) your managed website plan includes:

  • a professionally designed website
  • domain name registration and renewal
  • hosting and SSL certificate

  • basic SEO setup

Seeing your design first

We show you a design for your site at no cost and with no obligation. You don't pay anything until you've seen the design direction and decided you want to go ahead. If you choose not to proceed, you owe us nothing.

When payments start

Your first payment is due when you approve the design direction and give us the go-ahead to build and launch your site. Approval can be given by email, message, form submission, invoice payment, or another written go-ahead. Payments are monthly, in advance. We will agree on a payment method with you at that point.

You can cancel at any time by emailing us at hello@coastiewebsites.co.nz. There is no lock-in contract and no cancellation fee. Your service will continue until the end of the period you have already paid for, after which the managed website service will end.

What happens to your site if you cancel

Your monthly plan is an ongoing managed website service. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, the website design, layout, code and build remain owned by Coastie Websites, and your subscription gives you the right to use the website while your plan is active. If you cancel, the hosted website service will end after your paid period. You keep your domain name and any content you supplied, such as text, photos and logos, and we will provide reasonable help to transfer those to you.

Updates to your existing site are included as part of your monthly plan. This usually means changes such as wording, prices, hours, photos, testimonials, contact details, adding or removing sections, and keeping your content fresh. We aim to complete straightforward updates within 2 business days.

If you ask for something that is really a new feature or a major rebuild rather than an update, we'll talk it through first and agree what makes sense before any extra cost applies.
